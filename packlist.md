## Required Items

- **Cash!** Both huts we are staying at are cash only - make sure you have enough to cover lodging (62 euros will cover both nights for one person) PLUS extra to buy dinner & biers!
- **Hiking shoes / shoes with good traction:** you'll want a step up from athletic shoes for this route.  There are some areas of incline & rockier paths, so it will be especially important if we encounter rain!
- **Daypack - plan to leave the majority of your luggage in a car trunk or locker at the train station.** A 15-25L size back should suffice, you just need to be able to carry the main items listed below.
  - **Sleeping bag liner** ([example here](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B078GQVSMX/)) - the huts will provide a blanket & pillow, but you're required to bring one of these to slide into at night
  - **Lightweight/non-hiking slides or shoes** there is a no hiking-boot policy in the sleeping / common areas inside the huts.  would recco something light weight that you can rock with your wool socks
  - **Headlamp/small flashlight** to light your way if you need to use the restrooms after lights out
  - **General hiking attire** Things like a rain jacket 'just in case,' a warmer layer for hanging out at the huts at night- since we're at higher altitude it can get cold! Change of socks is always nice to have just in case.
  - **Toiletries** - both huts have washrooms with running water, however neither offer showers. You can bring wipes if you are so inclined!
  - **Water bladder or bottle** - You can refill with clean water along the way typically so it doesn’t need to be large.

## Optional items

- **Trekking Poles** - our first day of hiking will see up to a 30% grade as we approach the first hut. If you'd like to bring trekking poles, you can rent them from a sports shop in Oberstdorf.
- **Earplugs / eye mask:** if you're a light sleeper, you might want to bring these! Depending on when we check in, we could be in a larger dorm with bunks, or find ourselves in a 'one big bed' situation
